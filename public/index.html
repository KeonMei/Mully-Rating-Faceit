How the rating works

What counts

  • 80% combat: ADR, K/D, kills per round and MVPs.
  • 10% multikills: rounds with 2, 3, 4 or 5 kills.
  • 5% entry: opening duels won.
  • 5% clutch: clutches won compared with an average player.

Stats are added up over all matches, so one extreme game doesn't decide the result. Each stat is compared with an average FACEIT player. Full explanation →

Tiers

  • S · Strong 90–100
  • A · Great 75–89
  • B · Good 60–74
  • C · Average 45–59
  • D · Weak 0–44
